Frequently asked

Questions students ask before enrolling

Which is better: university or college?

Universities usually focus on degrees and research. Colleges and TVET institutions are great for practical, career-focused, shorter programs.

Can I switch courses after I enroll?

In some cases yes, if you meet the requirements and the institution approves the transfer. Always check faculty rules first.

How do I pick the right course?

Consider your interests, strengths, subjects, salary goals, job demand, and the type of work you enjoy every day.
